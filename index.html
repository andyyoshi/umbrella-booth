        Umbrella Booth — Engineering DFV project.
        The finished booth reads your student ID card directly. This page is the prototype stand-in and the public information page.
        The system stores your card ID, the booth, the slot number and the time. No name, no class, no photo, and the record is cleared when you return the umbrella. Location is optional and never leaves your phone.
